Privacy Policy

Graupel is a personal-use application operated by its owner through rclone.

Data Access and Use

The application requests Google Drive access to upload, list, inspect, verify, and manage files selected by its owner. Its authorization permits reading, creating, modifying, and deleting Google Drive files.

Data Storage and Security

OAuth credentials and operational records are stored on owner-controlled devices. Credentials are not intentionally published or made available to other users. Google Drive files remain subject to Google's storage and security practices.

Data Sharing

Google Drive data accessed through this OAuth client is used for the owner's file-management operations. It is not sold, used for advertising, or independently shared with third parties.

Retention and Revocation

Configuration and operational records are retained as needed to operate and maintain the application. The owner can delete local records and revoke the application's Google account access at any time.

Contact

Questions about this application can be directed to the user support contact identified on its Google OAuth consent screen.
